# Tuscany Bags

[Tuscany Bags](https://tuscanybagsusa.com) is an authorized retailer of Tuscany Leather, shipping authentic Italian leather bags and accessories from Florence, Italy to customers across the United States.

## Italian Leather Bags and Accessories

Tuscany Bags offers women's handbags and purses, totes, crossbody and shoulder bags, men's briefcases and laptop bags, backpacks, travel bags and duffels, wallets and leather accessories.

The products are Original Tuscany Leather products, cut and stitched by hand in the Florentine leather tradition. Every piece includes an authenticity certification tag and hologram.

## Shipping Across the United States

Tuscany Bags is an online store serving customers across the United States, including all 50 states.

Orders ship directly from Florence, Italy and typically arrive in 5 to 8 business days. Shipping is free on orders over US$400.

Prices are shown in US dollars.
